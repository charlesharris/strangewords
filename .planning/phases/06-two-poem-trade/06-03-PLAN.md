<objective>
Ship what the user sees. Reveal gains a quiet "trade back" affordance beside "let it go", driven by the server's trade fields through pure, test-pinned copy. Keepsakes become one coined name per completed poem, and the keepsake's category line is gone everywhere.

Locked decisions implemented here (06-CONTEXT.md):
- D-04: one coined name per poem, on device. Poem 1's name is coined from the reveal's lines as poem 2 begins (a trade never dissolves poem 1, so finishDissolving never sees it), and poem 2's at dissolution. Nothing new leaves the device.
- D-05: CoinedName's category field and its rendering line are removed for every poem, single poems included. The lead and the name stay.
- D-02's UI half: the offer lives on the Reveal screen beside "let it go", and the default path (doing nothing, or letting go) remains dissolution. This is the orchestrator default.

Discretion calls. No UI-SPEC exists for this phase, so the copy and layout below are planner choices, reversible and pinned by tests:
- Button: "trade back".
- Captions (human / robot poet):
  - partner asked: "the stranger would like to trade back" / "the robot poet would like to trade back"
  - your offer waiting: "waiting to see if the stranger trades back" / "the robot poet is thinking it over"
  - passed: "no trade back this time" / "no trade back with the robot poet this time"
- VoiceOver hints (human / robot poet):
  - offer: "Offers the stranger a second poem, the other way around." / "Offers the robot poet a second poem, the other way around."
  - accept: "Accepts the stranger's offer. A second poem begins, the other way around." / "Accepts the robot poet's offer. A second poem begins, the other way around."
- A VoiceOver announcement posts the caption when the state becomes "partner asked" or "passed".
- Two keepsakes render as two of today's lead-and-name blocks, stacked. After a trade, an interrupted ending (a partner left during poem 2) shows its farewell line above poem 1's name.

PoemNaming golden handling, a deliberate and documented step under the goldens' own header rule:
- Removing the category is a column removal, not a re-capture. Category strings are compile-time constants, never RNG draws, so every closure, its order, and every draw (beer style pick, perfume number, indie-band coin flip) stay in place. Every golden name and lead must therefore stay byte-identical.
- Duplicate name patterns that remain after the category goes (several coiners now share one shape) are NOT deduplicated, because that would change the catalogue weights and the names.
- The header note, the commit message and 06-03-SUMMARY.md record this. The SUMMARY section stands in for the Phase 4 fix ledger, which does not exist as a file yet.

Purpose: the user-facing half of the phase goal.
Output: TradeBack.swift and its tests, the Reveal affordance, the category removal with intact goldens, per-poem keepsakes, and the docs/STATUS.md keepsake paragraph.

Timing note: execute after Plan 02, and read every file fresh; the quick batch also changed AppModel.swift and the palettes. The tasks run sequentially (one pinned simulator).
</objective>
